06 / Evidence & reporting checklist

  1. Record the exact in-scope asset, endpoint, parameter, timestamp and account role.
  2. Keep the original request and response; redact session cookies, tokens and personal data.
  3. Show a minimal, harmless proof of concept. Avoid data extraction, persistence or destructive actions.
  4. For access-control issues, compare two accounts you control and prove only the authorization difference.
  5. Document impact, reproducibility, affected versions, remediation and program policy references.
